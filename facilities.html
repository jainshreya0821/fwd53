Lab Rules & Safety
  • Never look directly into a laser beam or a fiber end.
  • Wear safety goggles when working with lasers.
  • Handle lenses and mirrors only by their edges.
  • Do not bend optical fibers sharply; keep connector ends capped.
  • Students must work in the lab only in the presence of a supervisor.
